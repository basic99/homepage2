Thomas Asbridge, The Crusades: The Authoritative History of the War for the Holy Land, (2011). This is an account of the first crusade in the late 11th century, until the fall of the last crusader state at the end of the 13th century. It is interesting in that it does not just give history of the Latin crusaders but also the opposing Islamic powers.

The Path Between the Seas

David McCullough, The Path Between the Seas. The Creation of the Panama Canal 1870-1914, (New York, 1977). The Panama canal was something I had not given much thougt, but was a big story while it was being built. It was finished just as the World War was starting and the official opening was overshadowed by the news of war. The creation of the Panama state from part of Columbia by President Roosevelt is seen as the start of distrust of South American countries of the USA.
